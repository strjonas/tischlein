## Lift on power loss (26 Oct review: the balancer alone does not hold a payload)
  1 kg on the tray, power off, coils shorted: creeps down at ~1 mm/s (23 min for the full stroke)
  3 kg on the tray, power off, coils shorted: creeps down at ~3 mm/s (8 min for the full stroke)
  5 kg on the tray, power off, coils shorted: creeps down at ~5 mm/s (5 min for the full stroke)
  -> not a holding mechanism. Fix: a NEMA17 with a 24 V power-off (spring-applied) brake.
  brake 0.2 N*m at the motor -> holds 94 N at the carriage (5 kg parcel + a mis-set balancer needs 59 N: ok)
  brake 0.4 N*m at the motor -> holds 188 N at the carriage (5 kg parcel + a mis-set balancer needs 59 N: ok)
  Belt break: the balancer still carries the carriage; only a payload would come down (slowly, balancer friction).
  Bench test: 5 kg on the tray at 1.0 m, cut power, drift < 2 mm in 10 min; repeat with the brake wire pulled.

## Servo power on the bench (26 Oct review: the 12 V wiring was underspecified)
  every servo stalled at once: 17.7 A; realistic peak (two joints near stall, others moving): ~8 A
  -> the Waveshare adapter is a signal converter: route servo power around it, not through it.
     12 V 15 A supply -> 15 A main fuse -> latching e-stop -> fuse block: J1+J2 5 A, wrist (yaw, pitch, roll) 7.5 A,
     drawer 3 A; 1.0 mm2 wire to each branch; 2200 uF at the block; data + GND daisy-chained through the adapter.
     In every servo's EEPROM: protection current ~2 A (STS3215) / 3 A (STS3250), torque limit 80%, max temp 65 C.
     XL330 + U2D2 stay on their own 5 V supply; lift stepper on 24 V; common ground.
